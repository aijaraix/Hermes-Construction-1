# AEDRYX Public Landing Page Standard

## Objective

Create a professional public landing experience that explains what AEDRYX is before a user enters the authenticated construction workspace.

Current live root content still presents the application shell directly and uses the title "HERMES Construction". The public brand must present AEDRYX.

## Required public structure

### 1. Hero
- AEDRYX name/brand
- clear construction-intelligence headline
- concise subheadline
- primary CTA to enter/request access/demo as appropriate
- secondary CTA for product overview/contact
- premium OpenArt hero image or existing owner-approved hero
- no fake product screenshot

Suggested message direction:
"Construction intelligence that can see, plan, coordinate, and build in real space."

### 2. What AEDRYX is
Explain:
- real-scale construction digital twin;
- spatial reasoning;
- agent coordination;
- construction operations visibility;
- future-ready autonomy.

### 3. From site to structure
Explain the physical-world model:
- parcel/terrain;
- buildable/work envelope;
- excavation/foundation;
- structure/envelope;
- systems;
- completion.

### 4. Workfront orchestration
Explain:
- parallel workfronts;
- dependencies;
- materials;
- logistics;
- equipment;
- inspections;
- weather/environment constraints.

### 5. Visual construction twin
Show that the product lets humans see:
- what exists;
- what is happening now;
- what changed;
- what is next;
- what is blocked;
- where people/materials/equipment are.

Use real product screenshots only after they are genuinely rendered and accepted.

### 6. Building systems / x-ray
Explain coordinated structural, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, envelope and inspection views.

### 7. University / continual validation
Explain agent training, scenario validation, inspection, replay and evidence without overstating real-world autonomy.

### 8. Future robotics
Present as roadmap/future capability:
- spatially embodied agents/robots;
- payload/clearance reasoning;
- task sequencing;
- digital-to-physical execution.

Do not imply current live robotic construction if it is not physically deployed.

### 9. Final CTA
Clear route to demo/contact/access.

## Visual style

Use the existing AEDRYX dark construction-tech language:
- deep navy / graphite base;
- cobalt blue;
- violet;
- cyan;
- restrained warm orange for safety/attention accents;
- clean typography;
- high contrast;
- generous spacing;
- premium enterprise construction feel.

## Product/marketing truth

Concept art must be labeled/positioned as marketing imagery or editorial visual treatment.

Actual application views must come from the real AEDRYX renderer.

Do not blend concept art into fake UI frames that could be mistaken for product evidence.
