# Academy House 001 — Visual Reference Standard

## Purpose

Academy House 001 is the reference project for proving AEDRYX visual completeness before broad generalization.

It must function as a repeatable demonstration from empty site through construction-complete house.

## Reference requirements

The model must be recognizable as a house at all meaningful stages.

It must contain enough physically meaningful detail to prove:
- parcel/site relationship;
- foundation;
- walls;
- floor levels;
- openings;
- roof;
- framing;
- major MEP routes;
- permanent construction fixtures;
- exterior shell;
- completed building form.

## Architectural legibility

### Walls
- visible thickness;
- correct height;
- meaningful joins/corners;
- interior/exterior distinction where needed;
- hosted openings;
- non-orthogonal geometry supported where the design requires it.

### Roof
The roof may not be represented by a giant opaque rectangular box.

It must show the actual roof form:
- pitch;
- planes;
- ridge;
- hips/valleys where applicable;
- eaves/overhang;
- fascia/edge;
- roof covering layer;
- penetrations where modeled.

### Foundation
Show the actual foundation system and its relationship to terrain:
- excavation;
- footing/stem wall/slab geometry as applicable;
- forms/reinforcement in construction views where data exists.

### Openings
Doors/windows must appear as actual wall openings and not merely labels or overlapping boxes.

## Construction-stage visual sequence

### 0 — Existing/empty site
Parcel and terrain visible. Building not yet constructed.

### 1 — Site prep
Survey/layout/staging/access visibly established.

### 2 — Excavation
Ground visibly removed or graded where excavation occurs.

### 3 — Foundation
Foundation elements visibly installed in sequence.

### 4 — Framing
Structural members visible. Building silhouette emerges.

### 5 — Dry-in
Sheathing, windows/doors, weather barrier, roof covering visibly progress.

### 6 — Rough MEP
Major plumbing, electrical, HVAC routes visibly occupy the building.

### 7 — Close-in
Insulation/drywall/close-in visibly replace exposed framing where applicable.

### 8 — Construction finishes
Exterior cladding/roof/trim and permanent construction fixtures complete.

### 9 — Construction-complete
A professional, recognizable finished house is visible.

Furniture is not required.

## Acceptance heuristic

A builder viewing screenshots without being told the implementation details should not reasonably describe the scene as:
- “a bunch of boxes”;
- “massing only”;
- “placeholder geometry”;
- or “not recognizably a house.”

If they would, the reference standard has not been met.
