# Packet E — Building Systems

## Objective

Make major permanent building systems and construction fixtures visually understandable.

## Plumbing
Show meaningful routed geometry for:
- supply;
- DWV;
- stacks/vents where modeled;
- major fixture connections.

## HVAC
Show:
- major equipment;
- trunks/branches;
- registers/diffusers where modeled;
- condensate where relevant.

## Electrical
Show:
- service/panel;
- major routed raceway/wiring representation where useful;
- receptacles/switches/lights where modeled.

## Permanent construction fixtures

Include where canonical state supports them:
- toilets;
- sinks;
- showers/tubs;
- water heater;
- HVAC equipment;
- electrical panel;
- built-in mechanical/plumbing components.

Furniture and decoration are out of scope.

## Modes

Systems must be viewable without destroying architectural context:
- normal;
- MEP;
- x-ray;
- isolate selected system/component.

## Visual gate

A builder should be able to understand the approximate physical routing and placement of major systems and identify obvious spatial conflicts.
