# HERMES Autonomous Construction Gym & Curriculum

## Philosophy
The HERMES Gym continuously trains the system on progressively complex construction challenges across diverse geographic locations, codes, and climate hazards.

## Curriculum Levels
- **Level 1: Utility Building & Equipment Pad**: Simple slab-on-grade, small enclosure, basic gravity drainage.
- **Level 2: Single-Story Wood Frame Residence**: Standard residential framing, truss roofing, basic MEP rough-in.
- **Level 3: Coastal Hurricane Residence**: 150+ MPH wind loads, HVHZ impact resistance, elevated flood zone AE post-tensioned foundation.
- **Level 4: Cold Climate Townhome**: Zone 5A freeze-thaw cycles, 48" frost footings, party wall fire ratings, snow drift loads.
- **Level 5: Small Multi-Family Residential**: 3-story wood/masonry hybrid, elevator shaft framing, fire sprinkler zoning.
- **Level 6: Mid-Rise Concrete Hotel**: Post-tensioned concrete decks, MEP riser shafts, commercial curtain wall envelope.
- **Level 7: Commercial Core & Shell**: Structural steel framing, deep foundation piles, high-voltage electrical, complex HVAC chillers.

## Weakness Evaluator
When a project reaches completion, Prime evaluates category scores (Completeness, Structural, MEP, Environmental, Inspection, Cost) and automatically selects the weakest skill domain for the next training exercise.
