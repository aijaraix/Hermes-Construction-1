# HERMES CONSTRUCTION — MANAGER HIERARCHY & GOVERNANCE

## Overview
HERMES Construction relies on a strict management hierarchy to prevent individual specialist agents from making uncoordinated decisions. Discipline Managers and Spatial Managers govern bounded specialist trade roles, review proposals, enforce code rules, and manage trade interfaces.

---
