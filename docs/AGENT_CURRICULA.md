# HERMES CONSTRUCTION — AGENT-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE CURRICULA

## Overview
Every specialist agent role in HERMES Construction receives a dedicated, trade-specific **Knowledge Curriculum**. An agent's first job is to study its assigned curricula, ingest authoritative sources, extract domain rules, and demonstrate competency before participating in building construction.

---

## Discipline Curricula Breakdown

### 1. Site, Civil & Geotechnical Curricula
- **Topics**: Topography contouring, cut/fill balancing, soil bearing capacity (terzaghi equation, SPT N-values), silty sand behavior in coastal Florida, groundwater table fluctuations, stormwater retention ponds, NPDES silt fence installation, utility connection slopes.
- **Assigned Sources**: USDA Soil Surveys, FBC 2023 Chapter 18, FDOT Drainage Manual, Local Hillsborough County Public Works Standards.

### 2. Concrete & Structural Curricula
- **Topics**: ACI 318-19 concrete design, $f'_c = 4000\text{ PSI}$ mix designs with fly ash, water-cement ratios ($w/c \le 0.40$), ASTM A615 Grade 60 rebar development length and lap splices, continuous load path anchor bolt tension breakout ($N_{cb}$ calculations), ACI 301 concrete placement and curing.
- **Assigned Sources**: ACI 318-19, ACI 301, FBC 2023 Chapter 16/18, CRSI Manual of Standard Practice.

### 3. Wood & Engineered Framing Curricula
- **Topics**: USDA Wood Handbook (FPL-GTR-282), Southern Yellow Pine (SYP) design values, engineered I-joist rim board load transfer, hurricane tie uplift capacity (SIMPSON Strong-Tie load tables), diaphragm shear wall nail spacing, moisture content limits ($\le 19\%$).
- **Assigned Sources**: USDA FPL Wood Handbook (FPL-GTR-282), NDS National Design Specification for Wood Construction, APA Wood Frame Construction Guide.

### 4. Building Envelope & Roofing Curricula
- **Topics**: Building science heat/air/moisture movement, PNNL Building America Solution Center guides, FEMA P-55 coastal flashing details, 24-gauge AZ50 Galvalume standing seam panel wind uplift testing (TAS 125 / UL 580), 15-mil Stego Wrap vapor retarders (ASTM E1745 Class A).
- **Assigned Sources**: PNNL Building America Solution Center, FEMA P-55 Coastal Construction Manual, NRCA Roofing Manual.

### 5. MEP Systems Curricula
- **HVAC**: ACCA Manual J load calculations, Manual D duct sizing, static pressure drop math, ASHRAE 62.2 ventilation, condensate drain trapping.
- **Electrical**: NEC 2023 Article 210 GFCI/AFCI rules, feeder voltage drop ($\le 3\%$), panel load balancing, grounding electrode system (NEC Article 250).
- **Plumbing**: IPC 2023 fixture unit sizing (DFU/WSFU), sanitary drain slope requirements ($1/4"\text{ per foot}$ for $\le 2"$ pipe), trap seal protection, A.O. Smith heat pump water heater clearances.
