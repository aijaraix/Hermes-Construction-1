# HERMES CONSTRUCTION — UNIVERSAL MATERIALS ONTOLOGY

## Overview
HERMES Construction enforces deep material intelligence across **ALL major construction material families**. Materials are not treated as generic text labels. Every material item tracks chemical composition, mechanical properties, environmental degradation resistance, compatibility limits, supplier provenance, and intended use classifications (`INTENDED_USE_TYPE`).

---

## Intended Use Classification (`INTENDED_USE_TYPE`)

| Classification | Definition | Example Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `TEMPORARY_SACRIFICIAL` | Material used during construction and abandoned in place safely. | Formwork liner, sacrificial geotextile wrap. |
| `TEMPORARY_REUSABLE` | Temporary works equipment reused across multiple projects. | Trench shoring shields, scaffolding, temporary bracing. |
| `PERMANENT_INTERIOR` | Conditioned indoor space applications with low moisture/UV exposure. | Gypsum wallboard, interior doors, MDF trim. |
| `PERMANENT_EXTERIOR` | Unconditioned outdoor applications subject to rain, thermal cycling, UV. | Fiber cement siding, Galvalume standing seam roof. |
| `PERMANENT_BELOW_GRADE` | Continuous soil contact subject to hydrostatic pressure and groundwater chemistry. | Class 4000 PSI concrete footings, bituthene waterproofing. |
| `PERMANENT_WET_LOCATION` | Indoor or outdoor areas subject to frequent water splashing or steam. | Tile backer board, stainless steel shower drain fixtures. |
| `PERMANENT_COASTAL_MARINE` | Severe saltwater spray and salt air within 3 miles of coast. | **AISI Grade 316 Stainless Steel** connectors, hot-dip galvanized steel. |

## Material Family Taxonomy & Properties

### 1. Concrete & Cementitious
- **Grade/Spec**: 4000 PSI @ 28 Days, $w/c = 0.40$, Air Entrainment $4.5\% \pm 1.5\%$.
- **Corrosion Behavior**: High alkalinity ($pH \approx 12.5$-$13.0$) protects embedded rebar until carbonation or chloride ingress occurs.
- **Coastal Suitability**: Required Type II/V Portland cement for moderate sulfate resistance in coastal groundwater.

### 2. Fasteners & Structural Metals
- **Grade 316 Stainless Steel**: $16\text{--}18\%\text{ Cr}, 10\text{--}14\%\text{ Ni}, 2\text{--}3\%\text{ Mo}$. Exceptional resistance to pitting corrosion in marine coastal spray ($< 3.0\text{ miles}$ from salt water).
- **HDG (Hot-Dip Galvanized)**: $85\ \mu\text{m}$ zinc coating thickness ($G185 / Z600$). Suitable for exterior wood connectors beyond coastal splash zones.

### 3. Wood & Engineered Timber
- **Southern Yellow Pine (SYP) No. 2**: $F_b = 1,100\text{ PSI}$, $E = 1,400,000\text{ PSI}$. Pressure-treated with Koppers MicroPro MCA (Micronized Copper Azole) for ground contact ($0.15\text{ pcf}$ retention).
