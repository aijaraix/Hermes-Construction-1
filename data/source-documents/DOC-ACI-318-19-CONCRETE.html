[ACI 318-19 Building Code Requirements for Structural Concrete] CHAPTER 19: CONCRETE MATERIAL PROPERTIES Section 19.2.1: Compressive Strength Minimum specified compressive strength f'c for coastal foundation slabs exposed to brackish groundwater or soils is 4000 psi. Maximum water-cementitious materials ratio (w/cm) is 0.45. Section 26.5: Concrete Curing Structural concrete slabs must be continuously moist cured or coated with membrane-forming curing compound for a minimum of 7 consecutive days prior to loading.
